Project Statement: Hotel Management System

Problem Statement:
Hotels often face challenges in managing room bookings, guest records, room availability, and
billing manually. Without a proper system, these tasks can become time-consuming, error-prone,
and difficult to organize. This project aims to provide a structured and efficient solution for
handling core hotel operations.

Scope of Project:
This project focuses on a basic hotel management system that supports room management,
customer registration, booking records, billing, and daily operational tracking. The scope is
limited to essential front-desk and administrative functions that help manage hotel services
smoothly and efficiently.

Target Users:
This system is designed for hotel managers, receptionists, front desk staff, and administrators
who need to manage bookings, guest information, room occupancy, and payments. It is especially
useful for small to medium-sized hotels that require a simple and organized digital solution.

High-Level Features:
- Register and manage guest details
- Track room availability and room categories
- Handle booking, check-in, and check-out operations
- Store reservation information and customer records
- Generate bills and monitor payment status
- Maintain room occupancy and service updates
- Provide a simple overview of hotel activities for staff use

Overall Goal:
The main goal of the Hotel Management System is to simplify hotel operations, improve customer
service, reduce manual workload, and ensure efficient management of rooms, bookings, and billing.
